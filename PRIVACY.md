## 中文

### 一、概述

观音·祈福屏保（以下简称"本应用"）是一款离线运行的桌面屏保应用。**本应用不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人信息。**

### 二、数据收集

本应用**不**收集以下信息：

- 姓名、邮箱、电话等个人身份信息
- 位置信息
- 浏览记录、使用习惯
- 设备标识符

本应用不含广告 SDK、统计分析组件或任何第三方数据采集代码。

### 三、许可验证（唯一的外部通信）

若您从微软商店获取本应用，应用启动时会调用微软官方的商店许可接口（Windows.Services.Store API），仅用于验证您的授权状态（试用中 / 已购买）。

- 该验证在您的设备与微软商店服务之间进行，数据处理遵循 [微软隐私声明](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement)
- 本应用不会由此获取或存储您的任何个人信息
- 试用剩余时间由微软商店管理，本应用仅本地读取显示

### 四、本地数据

本应用仅在您的电脑本地保存以下数据：

- **偏好设置**（窗口置顶、声音开关等）：保存在本机应用数据目录
- **桌面快捷方式**（可选开启）

这些数据不离开您的设备，卸载应用后可随应用数据一并删除。

### 五、儿童隐私

本应用不面向儿童收集任何数据（实际上本应用不收集任何人的数据）。

### 六、政策变更

若本政策发生变更，将在本页面更新并修改"最后更新"日期。

### 七、联系与反馈

如果您有任何问题或建议，欢迎通过 GitHub 提交 Issue：

**https://github.com/pi-tiger/GuanyinBlessing/issues**

## English

### 1. Overview

Guanyin Blessing Screensaver (the "App") is an offline desktop screensaver. **The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

### 2. Data Collection

The App does **NOT** collect:

- Personal identity information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Location data
- Browsing history or usage habits
- Device identifiers

The App contains no advertising SDK, no analytics, and no third-party tracking code.

### 3. License Verification (the only external communication)

If you obtained the App from the Microsoft Store, the App calls the official Microsoft Store license API (Windows.Services.Store) at startup solely to verify your license status (trial / purchased).

- This verification happens between your device and Microsoft Store services, governed by the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement)
- The App does not receive or store any of your personal information through this
- Trial remaining time is managed by the Microsoft Store and only read locally for display

### 4. Local Data

The App stores the following data only on your local computer:

- **Preferences** (window always-on-top, sound toggle, etc.): in the local app data folder
- **Desktop shortcut** (optional)

This data never leaves your device and can be removed by uninstalling the App.

### 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from children (in fact, it collects no data from anyone).

### 6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, it will be updated on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

### 7. Contact & Feedback

If you have any questions or suggestions, please open an Issue on GitHub:
